What it proves (real MySQL, not mocks):
  - P12.01/P12.06 server-owned quote: the cart total is derived from the
    published price (2 x KSh 250.00 = 50000 minor), not from the browser.
  - P12.03 owner isolation: a different guest's quote is empty.
  - P12.02 no side effects: adding to a draft creates no charges, order_items
    or kitchen_tickets.
  - P12.03 expiry: an expired draft is dropped from the quote.
  - P12.06 digest stability: the quote digest is stable across reads.
  - P11.06/P12.07 permitted modifications: a fixed ingredient cannot be removed
    (VALIDATION_FAILED); a valid line is accepted.

Contract (P12.05): the cart GET responses now carry a typed `CartQuoteResponse`
(`CartQuote`/`CartQuoteLine`/`CartConflict`) in api/openapi.json, the JS client
is regenerated, and the contract validator asserts the shape.

Code fix (P12.07/P12.08): `CartService::update` no longer silently rebases a
line to the current published version/price when modifications are supplied; it
validates against the version the guest added and raises a `CART_CHANGED`
conflict if that version is withdrawn.
