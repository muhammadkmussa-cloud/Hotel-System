# Staff service handbook

Training draft based on proposed workflows. Replace example amounts/names with approved hotel training fixtures, never live customer data.

## Waiter

Open a visit at the correct table, add guests, and assign each tablet. Show the guest their table/guest label. Customers submit independently; check the table overview for coordination. Record changes through the system and kitchen acknowledgement, never by editing a previously printed ticket without recording the correction.

On cash payment, count money, enter tendered amount, return change, confirm the correct guest bill, and retain the payment reference. Hand cash to cashier through the handover screen. Closing a paid visit does not clear your remaining cash custody.

For card, direct payment to cashier. For M-PESA, help enter phone if requested; never ask for the PIN. If payment is uncertain, ask cashier to check the original attempt before prompting again.

## Cashier

Retrieve correct guest checkout or unpaid kiosk reference. For external card, confirm the terminal success and match its amount/reference before recording payment. For cash, use an active drawer or accept a waiter's handover after counting. Acknowledge only what was actually received.

Resolve outstanding/unknown payments using provider evidence, not a customer's screenshot alone. Keep refunds linked to originals and follow manager approval. At shift close, compare expected drawer, counted cash, terminal totals, provider collections, and unpaid/exception reports.

## Kitchen

Use table and guest labels together. Read modifications before starting. Each guest submission is a separate ticket; additional tickets do not replace the earlier meal unless explicitly marked as a correction. Mark preparation and readiness accurately. Shared charges do not mean several platters.

Allergy review belongs to the trained kitchen lead/service process. Do not assume a removed picture guarantees accommodation. Decline impossible changes and communicate an alternative for customer consent.

## Manager

Maintain staff permissions, recipe approval, availability, and escalation handling. Approve cancellations/refunds/discounts with reasons. Review cash differences and expired paid kiosk cases. Verify backups and daily unresolved exceptions. Do not clear an exception simply to make the dashboard green.

## End-of-service checklist

Resolve or transfer open visits; confirm remaining guest balances; review kitchen work; accept cash handovers; count drawers; reconcile card references and M-PESA transactions; inspect fiscal exceptions; check backup success; document unresolved issues for the next shift. Preserve history instead of deleting inconvenient records.
