# Food and ingredient asset handover

This is a template, not supplied production imagery. One row per approved asset; use separate rows for variants where needed.

| Asset ID | Meal/ingredient | Master filename | Rights/owner | Crop/focal point | Alt text and visible label | Chef/content approval | Variants complete |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| To assign | Actual catalogue item | To supply | Confirm permission | Record safe crop | Human-reviewed text | Name/date | Pending |

## Required checks

- Image represents the actual dish or ingredient and serving.
- Master image meets photography/crop targets in [asset specification](../design/03-food-assets.md).
- Recipe composition and image content agree; photo approval does not replace recipe review.
- Each ingredient remains recognisable at portrait size.
- Image dimensions and file size are recorded; responsive variants render correctly.
- Labels are independent of pixels and available in enabled languages.
- Asset licence permits the intended restaurant display/use; retain provenance.
- Demo assets are clearly separated and never silently published into a live menu.

## Recipe handover per meal

Meal name; description; actual base recipe; compound ingredients; permitted removals; extras and prices; portion choices; current selling price; preparation station; availability policy; review/allergy instructions; photographed serving; chef approver; publication version/date.
